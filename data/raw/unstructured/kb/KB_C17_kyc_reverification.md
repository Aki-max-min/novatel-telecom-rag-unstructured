# Completing KYC Re-Verification Requests

**Department:** Compliance  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-31  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Purpose

Steps for customers asked to complete KYC re-verification.

## Options

1. **Video KYC** — Account > KYC Update > Start Video KYC (ID + selfie liveness check).
2. **In-store** — visit any Experience Store with original ID and address proof.

## Consequences of Non-Compliance

Per licence conditions, non-completion within the notified window (typically 30 days) may result in outgoing service suspension, followed by full suspension.
