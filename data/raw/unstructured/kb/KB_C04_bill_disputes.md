## Purpose

Explains bill components and the dispute process for postpaid customers.

## Bill Components

- Base plan rental (pro-rated if mid-cycle change)
- Usage beyond plan allowance
- One-time/VAS charges
- Taxes (GST)
- Adjustments/credits from prior cycles

## Raising a Dispute

Use Billing > Dispute Charge, selecting the specific line item. Disputes must be raised within 90 days of bill generation per Policy_C04_billing_disputes.

## Resolution Timeline

Most disputes are resolved within 5-7 working days; complex usage investigations may take up to 15 working days.
