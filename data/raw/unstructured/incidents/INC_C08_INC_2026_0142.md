# Incident Report: 4G Cell Cluster Outage

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0142
**Category:** C08 — Network Coverage & Outages
**Region:** Mumbai West Circle
**Severity:** P1
**Department:** Network
**Date Logged:** 2026-04-30
**Status:** Resolved

## Summary
Fibre backhaul cut near Andheri due to third-party roadworks caused a 3-hour outage affecting ~40 4G sites.

## Timeline
06:12 IST fault detected via NOC alarms; 06:20 field team dispatched; 08:40 temporary microwave backhaul restored; 09:15 fibre repaired and primary path restored.

## Customer Impact
Estimated customers affected and service categories impacted are recorded in the internal incident-management system (ref: INC-2026-0142). Customer-facing status was published under Network > Service Status for the outage duration.

## Resolution
Service was restored per the timeline above. A follow-up Root Cause Analysis is tracked separately where applicable (see related_ids).

Preventive/corrective actions arising from this incident are tracked in the linked RCA document, where one was raised.
