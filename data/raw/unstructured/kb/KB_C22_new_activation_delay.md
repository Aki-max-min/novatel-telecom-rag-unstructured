# Diagnosing Delayed New SIM Activation

**Document ID:** KB_C22_new_activation_delay  
**Category:** C22 — New Connection Activation  
**Department:** Customer Care  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-06  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Source Authority:** Internal Knowledge Base

---

## Purpose

Root-cause checklist when a new SIM has not activated within the expected window.

## Checklist

- Confirm KYC document match (name/photo legible, address current)
- Check Activation Status in-app for a rejection reason code
- Confirm no duplicate active connection exists on the same ID exceeding regulatory connection limits

## Escalation

Delays beyond 48 hours (prepaid) or 72 hours (postpaid) should be escalated per SOP_C22_ACTIVATION_DELAY.
