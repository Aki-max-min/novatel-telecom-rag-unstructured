## Purpose

Explains what QoS metrics NovaTel publishes and what they mean.

## Key Metrics

- Call drop rate (benchmark: ≤2%)
- Complaint resolution within timeline (benchmark: ≥95% within SLA)
- Network availability (benchmark: ≥99%)

## Where Published

Quarterly circle-wise reports are published under Regulatory Disclosures on the NovaTel website, per TRAI's QoS regulations.
