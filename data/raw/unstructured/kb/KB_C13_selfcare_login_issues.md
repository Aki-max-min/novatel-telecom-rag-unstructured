## Purpose

Common login failure scenarios and fixes for the self-care app.

## Common Scenarios

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| OTP not received | SIM has no signal | Ensure signal, retry after 30s |
| OTP received late | SMS delivery delay | Use 'Login via Missed Call' |
| 'Account locked' | 5 failed attempts | Wait 30 min or reset via email link |

## Escalation Path

Persistent failures after all steps should be logged per SOP_C13_APP_ACCESS_ISSUES.
