# Incident Report: My NovaTel App Login Outage

**Document ID:** INC_C13_INC_2026_0184

**Customer Scope:** all
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-10

## Summary

An authentication microservice deployment introduced a certificate misconfiguration, blocking all app logins for 55 minutes.

## Detection Method

Detected automatically via NOC monitoring alarms, which triggered an on-call page within the standard 5-minute alerting SLA.

## Timeline

Deployment rolled back within 20 minutes of alert; certificate corrected; full service restored at T+55 minutes.
