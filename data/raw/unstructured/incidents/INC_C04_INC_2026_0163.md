# Incident Report: Duplicate Billing Cycle Run for Postpaid Segment

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0163
**Category:** C04 — Billing & Invoices
**Region:** Pan-India
**Severity:** P1
**Department:** Billing
**Date Logged:** 2026-07-04
**Status:** Resolved

## Summary
A scheduling error in the billing batch job caused ~12,000 postpaid customers to be billed twice for the June cycle.

## Timeline
Detected via automated reconciliation checks; batch job halted within 40 minutes; duplicate charges auto-reversed within 48 hours; affected customers notified via SMS.

## Customer Impact
Estimated customers affected and service categories impacted are recorded in the internal incident-management system (ref: INC-2026-0163). Customer-facing status was published under Network > Service Status for the outage duration.

## Resolution
Service was restored per the timeline above. A follow-up Root Cause Analysis is tracked separately where applicable (see related_ids).

## Preventive Actions
Preventive/corrective actions arising from this incident are tracked in the linked RCA document, where one was raised.
