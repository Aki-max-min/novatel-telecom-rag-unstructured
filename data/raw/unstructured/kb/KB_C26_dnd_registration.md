## Purpose

Explains DND registration options and the UCC (spam) complaint process.

## Registration

SMS 'START 0' to 1909 for full DND, or 'START' + category code(s) for partial blocking (e.g., banking/insurance/real-estate offers only).

## Reporting Spam Despite DND

Report via Help > Report UCC with date/time and calling number; repeated violations against a number are escalated to TRAI's UCC monitoring system.
