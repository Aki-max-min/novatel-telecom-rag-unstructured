## Eligibility & Activation

- Available to new and existing customers within the relevant customer scope (prepaid/postpaid/enterprise).
- Activation is instant on successful payment via the app, self-care portal, or an authorised retail outlet.

## Fair Usage Policy (FUP)

On exhausting the daily/period data allowance, speeds are reduced to the FUP rate for the remainder of that period; unlimited voice remains unaffected. See KB_C09_fup_data_speed for details.

## Roaming

Domestic roaming is included at no extra charge. International roaming requires a separate add-on pack; see the roaming category documentation.

## Renewal & Cancellation

- Plans can be renewed manually or via auto-renew (where enabled) before or on the expiry date.
- Cancellation before validity expiry does not entitle a pro-rata refund of the base plan price, except where required by policy.

## Taxes & Charges

All listed prices are exclusive of applicable government taxes (e.g. GST) unless stated as inclusive.

## Exceptions

Promotional or migrated legacy plans may carry different terms than shown here; the customer's active plan terms always take precedence over this catalogue.
