## Learning Objectives

Staff understand acceptable ID documents, video-KYC procedure, and consequences of KYC non-compliance.

## Acceptable Documents

Aadhaar (with consent), Passport, Voter ID, Driving Licence — must be original, legible, and unexpired.

## Video KYC Checklist

- [ ] Liveness check completed (customer performs a random action on request)
- [ ] Document held next to face, both clearly visible
- [ ] Geo-tag and timestamp captured automatically
- [ ] Customer consent to data processing explicitly recorded

## Consequences of Errors

Incomplete or fraudulent KYC exposes the company to regulatory penalties and licence risk — never bypass a required verification step, even under customer pressure.

## Key Definitions

- **SLA** — the target time within which a request or issue should be resolved.
- **Escalation** — handing a case to a specialist team when first-line steps do not resolve it.

## Worked Example

During a routine interaction, the agent identifies this scenario, applies the documented process, and confirms with the customer before closing the case.

## Common Mistakes

- Skipping identity verification before making account changes.
- Closing a case before the customer confirms the issue is resolved.

## Escalation

- Where first-line steps do not resolve the issue within the stated timeframe, the case is escalated to the next tier with full interaction history attached, and the customer is informed of the new reference and expected timeline.
