# Incident Report: UPI Recharge Gateway Timeout Spike

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0203
**Category:** C03 — Recharge & Payments
**Region:** Pan-India
**Severity:** P2
**Department:** Finance
**Date Logged:** 2026-07-19
**Status:** Resolved

## Summary
A payment-gateway partner experienced elevated latency causing a spike in recharge transactions stuck in 'Pending'.

## Timeline
Gateway partner notified; auto-reconciliation job run manually to clear backlog within 6 hours; all pending transactions resolved (credited or reversed).

## Customer Impact
Estimated customers affected and service categories impacted are recorded in the internal incident-management system (ref: INC-2026-0203). Customer-facing status was published under Network > Service Status for the outage duration.

## Resolution
Service was restored per the timeline above. A follow-up Root Cause Analysis is tracked separately where applicable (see related_ids).

## Preventive Actions
Preventive/corrective actions arising from this incident are tracked in the linked RCA document, where one was raised.
