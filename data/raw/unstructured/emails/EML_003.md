**Subject:** Your SIM Has Been Blocked as Requested

**From:** NovaTel Security Support <support@novatel.example>

**To:** Customer <registered-email-on-file>

## Context

This correspondence follows an earlier customer contact (call or app request) regarding the matter below, and reflects the outcome of the internal review.

## Previous Actions

- Customer request logged and assigned a reference number.
- Case reviewed by the relevant internal team against the applicable policy/SOP.

## Email Body

**Subject: Your SIM Has Been Blocked as Requested**

Dear Customer,

As per your request on our Fraud Helpline, your SIM (ending XXXX21) has been blocked with immediate effect to prevent unauthorised use. Please visit your nearest NovaTel Experience Store with a valid photo ID to collect a verified replacement SIM.

We also recommend informing your bank(s) of this incident if your number is linked to OTP-based transactions.

Stay safe,
NovaTel Security Team

## Resolution / Recommendation

The outcome above reflects the applicable policy and is considered final unless the customer provides new information within the stated window.

## Follow-up Requirements

The customer is invited to reply to this email or contact care with the reference number if further clarification is needed.
