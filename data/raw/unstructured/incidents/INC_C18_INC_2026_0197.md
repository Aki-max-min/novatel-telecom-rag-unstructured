# Incident Report: Phishing SMS Campaign Impersonating NovaTel

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0197
**Category:** C18 — Security & Fraud
**Region:** Pan-India
**Severity:** P1
**Department:** Security
**Date Logged:** 2026-07-16
**Status:** Resolved

## Summary
A third-party phishing campaign sent SMS messages impersonating NovaTel billing alerts with a malicious link.

## Timeline
Flagged via customer fraud reports; anti-fraud short code alert broadcast within 4 hours; domain reported for takedown; law-enforcement liaison initiated.

## Customer Impact
Estimated customers affected and service categories impacted are recorded in the internal incident-management system (ref: INC-2026-0197). Customer-facing status was published under Network > Service Status for the outage duration.

## Resolution
Service was restored per the timeline above. A follow-up Root Cause Analysis is tracked separately where applicable (see related_ids).

## Preventive Actions
Preventive/corrective actions arising from this incident are tracked in the linked RCA document, where one was raised.
