# Activating an eSIM on a Compatible Device

**Document ID:** MAN_ESIM_001
**Category:** C05 — SIM Card Services
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-22
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Requirements

An eSIM-compatible, network-unlocked device and an active NovaTel connection eligible for eSIM conversion.

## Steps

1. Request eSIM conversion via SIM Services > eSIM in the app.
2. Scan the QR code emailed/displayed upon approval using Settings > Add Cellular Plan.
3. Wait for activation confirmation (typically within 10 minutes).

## Notes

The physical SIM is deactivated automatically once the eSIM profile is confirmed active.
