## Purpose

Clarifies why data speeds reduce after a certain usage threshold.

## How FUP Works

Each plan specifies a high-speed data allowance (e.g., 1.5GB/day or 50GB/cycle). Once exceeded, speed is capped (commonly 64-512 kbps) for the remainder of the cycle rather than data being blocked.

## Checking Your FUP Status

My Plan > Data Usage shows consumption against your high-speed allowance in real time.
