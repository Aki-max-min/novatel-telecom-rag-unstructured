## Purpose

Describes the full mobile number portability journey for a customer switching to NovaTel.

## Process Steps

1. SMS 'PORT <10-digit number>' to 1900 to receive a Unique Porting Code (UPC).
2. Visit a NovaTel store or complete online porting request with the UPC and ID proof.
3. Choose a plan; submit KYC.
4. Await porting confirmation SMS (2 working days intra-circle, 4 working days inter-circle).
5. Insert the new NovaTel SIM once notified; the old SIM deactivates automatically.

## Common Rejection Reasons

- UPC expired (valid 15 days, 4 days if from J&K/Assam/NE circles)
- Outstanding dues with donor operator
- Ongoing request for change of ownership
