# Incident Report: UPI Recharge Gateway Timeout Spike

**Document ID:** INC_C03_INC_2026_0203
**Category:** C03 — Recharge & Payments
**Department:** Finance
**Customer Scope:** all
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-10
**Version:** 1.0
**Source Authority:** Internal Knowledge Base

---
## Summary

A payment-gateway partner experienced elevated latency causing a spike in recharge transactions stuck in 'Pending'.

## Detection Method

Detected automatically via NOC monitoring alarms, which triggered an on-call page within the standard 5-minute alerting SLA.

## Timeline

Gateway partner notified; auto-reconciliation job run manually to clear backlog within 6 hours; all pending transactions resolved (credited or reversed).

The initial working theory pointed to the most probable cause based on alarm signatures and affected-scope pattern; this was confirmed or revised during investigation.
