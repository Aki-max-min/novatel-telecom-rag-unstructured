# Incident Report: SIM Provisioning Delay Post-System Upgrade

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0171
**Category:** C05 — SIM Card Services
**Region:** Delhi NCR
**Severity:** P2
**Department:** Customer Care
**Date Logged:** 2026-06-07
**Status:** Resolved

## Summary
A provisioning-system upgrade introduced a queueing bug that delayed new SIM activations by up to 6 hours.

## Timeline
Issue identified via spike in activation-status complaint tickets; rollback of the upgrade completed within 3 hours; backlog cleared within 8 hours.

## Customer Impact
Estimated customers affected and service categories impacted are recorded in the internal incident-management system (ref: INC-2026-0171). Customer-facing status was published under Network > Service Status for the outage duration.

## Resolution
Service was restored per the timeline above. A follow-up Root Cause Analysis is tracked separately where applicable (see related_ids).

## Preventive Actions
Preventive/corrective actions arising from this incident are tracked in the linked RCA document, where one was raised.
