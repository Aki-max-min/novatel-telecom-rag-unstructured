# Incident Report: FTTH Regional Outage — Fibre Cut

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0179
**Category:** C25 — Broadband & FTTH Services
**Region:** Pune Circle
**Severity:** P1
**Department:** Network
**Date Logged:** 2026-05-09
**Status:** Resolved

## Summary
A construction excavation severed a primary fibre trunk affecting ~2,800 broadband customers.

## Timeline
Outage detected 14:02 IST; splicing team dispatched; service restored 19:45 IST after trunk repair and re-splicing.

## Customer Impact
Estimated customers affected and service categories impacted are recorded in the internal incident-management system (ref: INC-2026-0179). Customer-facing status was published under Network > Service Status for the outage duration.

## Resolution
Service was restored per the timeline above. A follow-up Root Cause Analysis is tracked separately where applicable (see related_ids).

Preventive/corrective actions arising from this incident are tracked in the linked RCA document, where one was raised.
