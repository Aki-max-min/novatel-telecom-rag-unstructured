**Department:** Customer Care
**Category:** General (C01-C29)
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-21
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, agents can explain bill components, identify common dispute types, and apply the correct SOP.

## Scenario 1

**Customer:** "Why is my bill higher this month?"
**Correct Approach:** Pull up the itemised bill, walk through each line item, check for plan changes or roaming usage, and offer to raise a dispute if the customer disagrees with a specific charge.

## Scenario 2

**Customer:** "I was charged for a VAS I never subscribed to."
**Correct Approach:** Check subscription history for the activation channel (SMS/USSD/app click). If consent cannot be verified, process a refund per SOP_C10_VAS_DISPUTE and deactivate the service.

## Assessment Checklist

- [ ] Agent verified customer identity before discussing account details
- [ ] Agent explained charges in plain language
- [ ] Agent offered next steps (dispute/refund/escalation) where appropriate
