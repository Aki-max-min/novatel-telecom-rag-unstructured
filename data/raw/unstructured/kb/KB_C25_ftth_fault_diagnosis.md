## Purpose

First-line diagnostic guide for home fibre broadband connectivity issues.

## ONT Light Reference

| Light | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green PON | Signal OK |
| Red PON / LOS | Loss of signal — fibre cut or loose connector |
| Off | Power issue |

## First Steps

1. Check power connection to ONT.
2. Check fibre connector is fully seated (do not bend fibre sharply).
3. If LOS persists, log a fault ticket — do not open the ONT casing.
