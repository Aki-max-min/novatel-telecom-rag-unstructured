# Incident Report: Data Speed Degradation During IPL Match

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0158
**Category:** C09 — Data Services & Speed
**Region:** Bengaluru Circle
**Severity:** P2
**Department:** Network
**Date Logged:** 2026-03-24
**Status:** Resolved

## Summary
Localised congestion at 6 sites near a stadium during a high-footfall event caused sustained data speed drops.

## Timeline
Congestion alarms triggered at 19:05 IST; temporary capacity boost via portable COW (Cell on Wheels) deployed by 20:10; normal service resumed post-event.

## Customer Impact
Estimated customers affected and service categories impacted are recorded in the internal incident-management system (ref: INC-2026-0158). Customer-facing status was published under Network > Service Status for the outage duration.

## Resolution
Service was restored per the timeline above. A follow-up Root Cause Analysis is tracked separately where applicable (see related_ids).

Preventive/corrective actions arising from this incident are tracked in the linked RCA document, where one was raised.
