## Purpose

This article explains how prepaid plan validity is calculated, where to check it, and what happens at expiry.

## Checking Your Plan Validity

1. Open the My NovaTel app.
2. Go to **My Plan**.
3. The expiry date/time is shown under 'Validity'.

## What Happens at Expiry

On expiry, outgoing services stop. Incoming calls typically continue for a grace period (15-90 days depending on plan type) before the number enters the deactivation cycle.
