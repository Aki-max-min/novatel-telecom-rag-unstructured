## Getting Started

Download from the Play Store/App Store, log in with your registered mobile number, and verify via OTP.

## Key Features

- Recharge & bill payment
- Usage tracking (data/voice/SMS)
- VAS management
- Complaint lodging and tracking
- Roaming pack activation
