## Context

This message is sent as a follow-up after the customer's original query was logged and investigated by the relevant team.

## Previous Actions

- Customer request logged and assigned a reference number.
- Case reviewed by the relevant internal team against the applicable policy/SOP.

## Email Body

**Subject: Update on Your Data Speed Concern**

Dear Customer,

Thank you for reporting slow data speeds. On review, your account had crossed the high-speed data allowance under your current plan, triggering Fair Usage Policy throttling. This resets automatically on your next billing cycle date.

If you require additional high-speed data before then, Data Booster packs are available under Recharge > Add-ons in the app.

Regards,
NovaTel Technical Support

## Resolution / Recommendation

The outcome above reflects the applicable policy and is considered final unless the customer provides new information within the stated window.

## Follow-up Requirements

The customer is invited to reply to this email or contact care with the reference number if further clarification is needed.
