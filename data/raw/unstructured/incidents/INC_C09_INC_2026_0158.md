# Incident Report: Data Speed Degradation During IPL Match

**Document ID:** INC_C09_INC_2026_0158
**Category:** C09 — Data Services & Speed
**Department:** Network
**Customer Scope:** all
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-12

## Summary

Localised congestion at 6 sites near a stadium during a high-footfall event caused sustained data speed drops.

## Detection Method

First flagged by a spike in customer-reported tickets and self-care app status checks, then confirmed against NOC monitoring dashboards.

## Timeline

Congestion alarms triggered at 19:05 IST; temporary capacity boost via portable COW (Cell on Wheels) deployed by 20:10; normal service resumed post-event.
