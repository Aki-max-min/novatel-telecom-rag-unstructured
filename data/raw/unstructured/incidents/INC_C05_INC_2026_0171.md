# Incident Report: SIM Provisioning Delay Post-System Upgrade

**Document ID:** INC_C05_INC_2026_0171
**Category:** C05 — SIM Card Services
**Department:** Customer Care
**Customer Scope:** all
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-31
**Version:** 1.0
**Source Authority:** Internal Knowledge Base

## Summary

A provisioning-system upgrade introduced a queueing bug that delayed new SIM activations by up to 6 hours.

## Detection Method

Detected automatically via NOC monitoring alarms, which triggered an on-call page within the standard 5-minute alerting SLA.

## Timeline

Issue identified via spike in activation-status complaint tickets; rollback of the upgrade completed within 3 hours; backlog cleared within 8 hours.

| Time | Event | Observation | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| T+0 | Fault detected | Alarm/ticket volume triggers investigation | On-call engineer paged |
| T+10m | Initial triage | Affected scope estimated | Field/remote team engaged as needed |
| T+Xh | Interim mitigation | Partial service restored where possible | Status page updated |
| T+Yh | Full restoration | Service confirmed normal | Incident closed, RCA initiated if required |

## Customer Impact

Estimated customers affected and service categories impacted are recorded in the internal incident-management system (ref: INC-2026-0171). Customer-facing status was published under Network > Service Status for the outage duration.

## Initial Hypothesis

The initial working theory pointed to the most probable cause based on alarm signatures and affected-scope pattern; this was confirmed or revised during investigation.

## Investigation

The response team correlated alarm data, affected-site/customer patterns, and any recent change or third-party activity in the area to isolate the fault to a specific component or cause before applying a fix.

## Resolution

Service was restored per the timeline above. A follow-up Root Cause Analysis is tracked separately where applicable (see related_ids).

## Recovery Validation

Restoration was confirmed via monitoring returning to baseline and, where applicable, spot checks with affected customers before the incident was formally closed.
