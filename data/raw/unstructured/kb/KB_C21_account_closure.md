## Purpose

Covers both voluntary closure and reactivation after non-payment deactivation.

## Voluntary Closure

1. Clear outstanding dues.
2. Submit closure request (app or store).
3. Final bill generated; number enters cooling-off period.

## Reactivation After Non-Payment

Possible within the retention window (commonly 90 days) by clearing dues; may require re-KYC if the gap exceeds regulatory thresholds.
