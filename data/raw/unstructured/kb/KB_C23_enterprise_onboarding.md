## Purpose

High-level overview of onboarding a new enterprise/business customer.

## Stages

1. Commercial agreement & credit assessment
2. Bulk KYC collection for all end-user connections
3. Provisioning (numbers, APNs, centralised billing setup)
4. Assignment of dedicated Enterprise Account Manager
5. Go-live and SLA activation
