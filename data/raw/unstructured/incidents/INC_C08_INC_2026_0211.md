# Incident Report: 5G Site Cluster Down Due to Power Outage

**Document ID:** INC_C08_INC_2026_0211
**Category:** C08 — Network Coverage & Outages
**Department:** Network
**Customer Scope:** all
**Last Updated:** 2026-04-15
**Version:** 1.0
**Source Authority:** Internal Knowledge Base

---
## Summary

A regional grid power outage without adequate battery backup runtime caused 14 5G sites to go offline.

## Detection Method

First flagged by a spike in customer-reported tickets and self-care app status checks, then confirmed against NOC monitoring dashboards.

## Timeline

Sites reverted to 4G-only via automatic failover where power allowed; generator backup deployed to worst-affected sites within 5 hours; full restoration within 9 hours.

## Customer Impact

Estimated customers affected and service categories impacted are recorded in the internal incident-management system (ref: INC-2026-0211). Customer-facing status was published under Network > Service Status for the outage duration.

## Initial Hypothesis

The initial working theory pointed to the most probable cause based on alarm signatures and affected-scope pattern; this was confirmed or revised during investigation.

## Investigation

The response team correlated alarm data, affected-site/customer patterns, and any recent change or third-party activity in the area to isolate the fault to a specific component or cause before applying a fix.

## Resolution

Service was restored per the timeline above. A follow-up Root Cause Analysis is tracked separately where applicable (see related_ids).

## Recovery Validation

Restoration was confirmed via monitoring returning to baseline and, where applicable, spot checks with affected customers before the incident was formally closed.
