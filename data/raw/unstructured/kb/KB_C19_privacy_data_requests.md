# Handling Customer Data Access and Deletion Requests

**Document ID:** KB_C19_privacy_data_requests  
**Category:** C19 — Privacy & Data Protection  
**Department:** Legal  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-23  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Source Authority:** Internal Knowledge Base

---

## Purpose

Explains the process for a customer requesting a copy or deletion of their personal data.

## Process

1. Customer submits request via Privacy Settings > My Data.
2. Identity is verified.
3. Data export/deletion is actioned within the regulatory timeline (typically 30 days).
4. Certain records (e.g., Call Detail Records) are retained per statutory minimum retention periods even after deletion requests, per Policy_C19_privacy_policy.
