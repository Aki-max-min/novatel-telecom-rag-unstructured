# Incident Report: My NovaTel App Login Outage

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-0184
**Category:** C13 — App & Self-Care Portal
**Region:** Pan-India
**Severity:** P1
**Department:** Digital Channels
**Date Logged:** 2026-03-15
**Status:** Resolved

## Summary
An authentication microservice deployment introduced a certificate misconfiguration, blocking all app logins for 55 minutes.

## Timeline
Deployment rolled back within 20 minutes of alert; certificate corrected; full service restored at T+55 minutes.

## Customer Impact
Estimated customers affected and service categories impacted are recorded in the internal incident-management system (ref: INC-2026-0184). Customer-facing status was published under Network > Service Status for the outage duration.

## Resolution
Service was restored per the timeline above. A follow-up Root Cause Analysis is tracked separately where applicable (see related_ids).

## Preventive Actions
Preventive/corrective actions arising from this incident are tracked in the linked RCA document, where one was raised.
