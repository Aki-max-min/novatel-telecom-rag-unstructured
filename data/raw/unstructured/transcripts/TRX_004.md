Agent: NovaTel Sales, this is Arjun, how can I help?
Customer: I want to port my number from another operator to NovaTel.
Agent: Sure! First, please SMS 'PORT' followed by your 10-digit number to 1900 from that number. You'll receive a Unique Porting Code, or UPC.
Customer: Okay, got it — it says PQR456.
Agent: Great, that code is valid for 15 days. You can now visit any NovaTel store with this UPC and a valid ID proof to complete the porting request.
Customer: How long before it's active?
Agent: Typically 2 working days for porting within the same telecom circle, or 4 working days if it's a different circle.
Customer: Perfect, thank you!
